THE IMPACT LOOP

How value moves through SUTRA-GEO

Explore→Learn→Earn→Redeem→Local partner benefits

INNOVATION

Why can’t a normal national tourism directory simply add this?

The answer demonstrated by the prototype is the connected loop itself: location trigger → interaction → personal memory → community participation → measurable impact.

Location-aware

The experience changes when a traveller physically enters a heritage zone.

Interactive

A place becomes a micro-quest instead of a passive information card.

Personalized

Passport, media and journey history turn exploration into a personal cultural record.

Community-connected

Stories and artisan experiences connect discovery to local knowledge and people.

Reward-driven

Sutra Coins create a visible loop from participation to local rewards.

AI as support

Ask Sutra explains the selected node and surfaces the source instead of claiming generic AI as the entire innovation.

SUSTAINABILITY & PARTNERSHIPS

Who can make the loop sustainable?

Ministry & tourism boards

Curated heritage circuits can complement official destination information and state tourism programs.

ASI & heritage institutions

Source-backed nodes can point travellers toward authoritative heritage information and conservation context.

Artisans & local businesses

Experience inquiries create a path from discovery to paid local participation.

Open commerce

Future booking or commerce flows can integrate with open networks such as ONDC rather than building a closed marketplace.

Partnerships are proposed pathways, not current integrations. The prototype does not claim government or ONDC onboarding.

PRODUCTION READINESS

What changes beyond the prototype?

Browser prototype→Authenticated API→PostGIS geofencing→Server-side quest verification→Moderation + analytics

The repository includes a backend/database scaffold, but the deployed demo remains intentionally client-side. No API key or fake hosted backend is shipped in the browser.
